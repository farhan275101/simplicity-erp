Simplicity ERP Ka Complete Digital Solution

Zameen se juda software jo aapke karkhane ke har mod par maal aur paise ko 100% lock kar deta hai.

MODULE 1

🧵 Kariger Master & Tani Khata

Har karigar ki Tani sheet ka exact hisab. Manual Rate (Bana, Zari, Bani) aur auto-formula deduction.

  • ✔️ Net Bana = Cone - Less TP
  • ✔️ Net Zari = Qty - Topi
  • ✔️ Bana & Zari Wastage Deduction
  • ✔️ 1-Click WhatsApp Khata Parchi
MODULE 2

🧶 Raw Material & Sancha Stock

Main Godam aur sabhi Branch Karkhanon me bache huye Bana, Zari aur Sanche ka live stock balance.

  • ✔️ Godam-wise Multi-Karkhana Tabs
  • ✔️ Sancha Item with Person Name & Search
  • ✔️ Karigar Returning Maal Auto-Add (+)
  • ✔️ Stock Out Par Negative Alert Warning
MODULE 3

🥻 3-Process Saree Stock

Saree ka har roop alag register me darj hota hai taaki kachhi aur paki saree aapas me mix na ho.

  • ✔️ Fresh Saree (Loom Weave Inward)
  • ✔️ Finished Saree (Calender Se Tayyar)
  • ✔️ Lott / Damage Saree Ka Alag Khata
  • ✔️ Passbook: Hara (Aayi) aur Laal (Main Gayi)
MODULE 4

✂️ Job Work Hub (Cutting & Calender)

Batch Entry System jisme 50 karigar ka maal 1 single line me darj ho jata hai bina register bhare.

  • ✔️ Cutting: Sirf Main Godam Fresh Stock Se
  • ✔️ Calender: Fresh Ya Cutting Se Aayi Saree
  • ✔️ Live Register: Cutting Aayi, Calender Gayi
  • ✔️ Job Work Bills, Katuti & Payment Raseed
MODULE 5

🏪 Apni Dukan Bheja (Final Out)

Karkhane se tayyar maal dukan ya gaddi par bhejte waqt ka digital parcel register.

  • ✔️ Calender Se Tayyar Saree Ka Live Out
  • ✔️ Tareekh-wise Challan & Driver Note
  • ✔️ Karkhane me kitna maal bacha, live update
MODULE 6

📅 1 April Year-End Closing (Naya Saal)

Pura saal hamesha ke liye सुरक्षित (Locked). Naye saal me app bilkul halka aur fresh khulega.
