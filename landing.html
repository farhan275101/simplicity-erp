Complete Karkhana Workflow

Loom Se Lekar Dukan Tak Har Kadam Digital

Bina kisi password ke direct kisi bhi feature par click karein aur uska Ultra-HD interactive layout dekhein.

⚡ SUPERFAST

⚡ Kariger Express (बैठक काउन्टर)

Baithak ke din 1-screen par Zero-Mouse hisab. Saree jama, Bana/Zari issue, Mazdoori rokad aur Tani progress ek sath.

  • ✔️ 100% Zero-Mouse (Enter dabate jao agla box aayega)
  • ✔️ 72 Pcs Tani Target & Loom par bachi saree live track
  • ✔️ 🎨 8 Colors matching list display
  • ✔️ Enter dabate hi turant A6 Thermal parchi print
⚡ INWARD COUNTER

⚡ Stock Express (कच्चा माल आवक)

Mandi ya tempo se aayi 10-15 bori Bana, Zari aur Sancha ek hi bade counter par bina screen badle 1 minute me stock me jama.

  • ✔️ 3-in-1 Engine: Bana, Zari aur Sancha
  • ✔️ [F1/F2/F3] Shortcuts se instant category switch
  • ✔️ Bacha wazan live screen par dikhna (502 Kg Bacha)
  • ✔️ Purana Stock ya +Naya Item mode
STEP 01

🧵 Kariger Master & Tani Khata

Har kariger ki Tani 1, 2, 3 sheets ka exact hisab. Mazdoori (Bani rate), 8 Colors matching aur Sancha record.

  • ✔️ 72 Saree Tani & Chukka Saree Setting
  • ✔️ Net Mazdoori = Net Saree × Bani Rate
  • ✔️ 15/30 din se absent kariger ka alert
STEP 02

🧶 Tana, Bana & Zari Issue

Kariger ko diye gaye daage ka pakka hisab. Less TP aur Topi ka wazan minus karke exact net issue nikalein.

  • ✔️ Net Bana = Cone - Less TP
  • ✔️ Net Zari = Qty - Topi (Wastage cut)
  • ✔️ Kariger return maal foran godam me jama
STEP 03

⚙️ Sancha (Tani & Chukka) System

Tani beam aur Chukka ka karkhana-wise alag-alag Kg hisab. Kis kariger ke paas kaun sa sancha hai, live tracking.

  • ✔️ Tani aur Chukka ke 2 alag-alag tabs
  • ✔️ Person-wise wazan ka hisab
  • ✔️ Godam se sancha issue hone par auto-minus
STEP 04

🥻 Fresh Saree Inward & Godam

Loom se utari gayi kachhi saree seedha Fresh Register me aati hai. Main Godam, Bahadurganj aur Mubarakpur ka alag hisab.

  • ✔️ Kariger sheet se nikal kar stock me aana
  • ✔️ Fresh, Cutting, Finished & Lott 4 registers
  • ✔️ 1 Godam se doosre godam transfer challan
STEP 05

✂️ Booti Cutting (Job Work)

Fresh stock se saree cutting ke liye bhejna. Single-line batch entry me sabhi kariger ka maal ek sath chadhayein.

  • ✔️ Kisko kitni saree di aur kab wapas aayi
  • ✔️ Damage saree aur cutting fine / kaat darj karein
  • ✔️ Cutting wale kariger ka monthly khata
STEP 06

🎛️ Calender Machine Finish (Ironing)

Badi-badi steam roller press machine me saree bhejna taaki unki uncreased fall aur silky chamak tayyar ho sake.

  • ✔️ Cutting se ya Fresh se Calender bhejna
  • ✔️ Calender Finished hote hi final stock ready
  • ✔️ Calender bills & payment voucher
STEP 07

🏪 Dukan Bheja (Final Out Register)

Karkhane se tayyar saree dukan ya wholesale gaddi par bhejna. Karkhane me kitni bachi aur dukan kitni gayi, live status.

  • ✔️ Date-wise parcel challan & driver note
  • ✔️ Karkhane se final minus hokar out hona
  • ✔️ Pure saal me dukan par kitni saree gayi summary
STEP 08

💳 Payment & Bakaya System

Kariger ko diya gaya advance ya payment turant uske khate me darj karein. Kul Dena Baki aur Diya gaya payment saaf dikhega.

  • ✔️ Cash, Online UPI ya Bank payment modes
  • ✔️ 1-Click WhatsApp par Digital Parchi bhejein
  • ✔️ Kariger aur Maalik dono ke mobile me same hisab
STEP 09

📅 1 April Naya Saal (Year Closing)

2000 Kariger ka pichla bacha hisab naye saal me minton me transfer, naye sire se diary likhne ki koi zaroorat nahi.

  • ✔️ Har godam ka bacha maal ➔ Naya Opening Stock (100% Match)
  • ✔️ Kariger baki mazdoori ➔ Tani 1 me carry-forward
  • ✔️ Purane saal me kabhi bhi switch karke dekhne ki azaadi
